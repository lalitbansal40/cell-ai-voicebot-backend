# Compliance Research Notes (T0.18)

> ⚠️ **Research notes, not legal advice.** Rules change often (several items below are drafts). Everything here must be confirmed with the client and their legal / compliance team before go-live. Checked on **2026-10-08**.

Confidence: **high** = read on an official source · **medium** = official source referenced via reputable secondary sources · **low** = secondary sources only / draft / unclear.

## 1. TRAI — commercial calls (TCCCPR 2018 + 2025 amendment)

| Rule                   | Summary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Confidence      |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Number series          | **Promotional** calls must use the **140xx** series; **service / transactional** calls by **BFSI regulated entities** (RBI / SEBI / IRDAI / PFRDA) must use the **1600xx** series. Ordinary 10-digit numbers must not be used for telemarketing. | [TRAI PR 11/2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf); [SCC Online on 1600/140](https://www.scconline.com/blog/?p=390749)                                                                                                                | medium          |
| 1600 series protection | No tagging, blocking or filtering of 1600-series calls by operators or third-party apps.                                                                                                                                                         | [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/?p=390749)                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | medium          |
| DND / preferences      | Customers block promotional calls via the DND registry (NCCPR); service/transactional calls need a relationship or consent. Explicit transactional consent valid **7 days**; inferred consent only during the contractual relationship.          | [JSA Prism Feb 2025](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/JSA-Prism-Telecommunications_February-2025_TCCCPR-amendments.Final_.pdf); [Bar & Bench, 2026-03-09](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/trais-crackdown-on-spam-calls-and-ai-driven-telemarketing) | medium          |
| Registration           | Entities making commercial calls register with operators on the DLT platform (principal entity, headers, consent templates).                                                                                                                     | JSA Prism; Bar & Bench                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | medium          |
| Calling hours          | Unsolicited commercial communication only **09:00–21:00 IST**.                                                                                                                                                                                   | [Exotel calling-hours FAQ](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/calling-hours)                                                                                                                                                                                             | low (secondary) |
| Enforcement            | Complaint window 7 days; action at 5 complaints in 10 days; operators may suspend all telecom resources of repeat violators.                                                                                                                     | Bar & Bench; [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/446114/trai-tcccpr-2025-tougher-rules-spam-calls-sms.html)                                                                                                                                                           | medium          |
| AI telemarketing       | TRAI is **considering** amendments for AI-based telemarketing, AI disclosures and consent verification — not in force.                                                                                                                           | Bar & Bench, 2026-03-09                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | low             |

**Product impact:** loan-recovery calls are **service calls of an RBI-regulated lender** → the client must place them from a **1600-series** number (caller ID on the SIP trunk). Promotional campaigns (if ever) need 140-series + DND scrubbing.

## 4. Call recording

- No single Indian statute found that requires consent for a participant to record a call; **best practice** (and the 2026 RBI draft) is to **inform** the borrower at the start: e.g. _"Yeh call quality aur record ke liye record ki ja rahi hai."_ — confidence **low**; confirm with legal.
- Store recordings encrypted, signed-URL access only, retention per client policy.

## 5. AI disclosure

- **No Indian rule in force** found that mandates disclosing an AI/automated caller; TRAI is **considering** AI-disclosure amendments (Bar & Bench, 2026-03-09). Confidence **low**.
- Recommendation: **disclose by default** (account setting `aiDisclosureEnabled = true`, already in the data model): _"Main <Company> ki taraf se AI assistant bol rahi hoon."_ — it is already in the PoC persona.

## 7. Questions for the client's legal / compliance team

1. Which licence does the lender hold (bank / NBFC / HFC / other), and which RBI recovery directions apply to them today?
2. Will calls be placed from a **1600-series** number registered to the lender? Who handles DLT / number registration?
3. Confirm the calling window for recovery calls (we default to 09:00–19:00).
4. Maximum call attempts per borrower per day / week?
5. Must every call be recorded? What intimation wording should the bot say? Retention period for recordings and transcripts?
6. Do you want the bot to disclose it is an AI assistant? Exact wording?
7. Lawful basis under DPDP for contacting borrowers (consent / legitimate use) — and for sending voice data to OpenAI (US)? Any data-localisation constraints?
8. Can the bot discuss the amount and dues with whoever answers, or must it first verify the borrower's identity (e.g. date of birth / last 4 digits)?
9. Escalation: when must a human agent take over (dispute, hardship, complaint)?
10. Who signs the DPA, and what breach-notification timeline do you need from us?
