**What goes to OpenAI (Phase 5, AI agents):** the compiled agent instructions (persona, rules), only the contact variables the client marked as allowed (plus the last 4 digits of the phone when allowed), the conversation, retrieved knowledge chunks and tool results. Full phone numbers, external ids and function secret headers stay on our server (functions fill them server-side). OpenAI is a **sub-processor** (API data not used for training; up to 30 days abuse-monitoring retention — request **Zero Data Retention** for production, docs/setup/openai.md §2). Every agent starts with an AI disclosure line (§5), editable but on by default.

**Product impact:** DPA template with the client, sub-processor list (OpenAI, AWS, telephony provider), retention jobs + delete-on-request, PII encryption & log masking, breach runbook, audit logs, data export per account.

**Call flows (Phase 6):** the four flow templates open with the AI disclosure ("…ki taraf se ek AI assistant bol rahi hoon"), use polite Hinglish with no threats or shaming, offer a callback / transfer path, and end politely on silence or wrong keys (`callback_requested`). The calling-hours block (08:00–19:00) is enforced by campaigns (Phase 8), not inside a flow. Flow templates are starting points — the client must review the script (open question in PHASE_6_PLAN §7).

## 4. Call recording

- No single Indian statute found that requires consent for a participant to record a call; **best practice** (and the 2026 RBI draft) is to **inform** the borrower at the start: e.g. _"Yeh call quality aur record ke liye record ki ja rahi hai."_ — confidence **low**; confirm with legal.
- Store recordings encrypted, signed-URL access only, retention per client policy.
- **Phase 7 built:** every answered call starts with the account's **recording notice** (Settings → Calls; default _"Yeh call quality ke liye record ki ja rahi hai."_, ≤ 200 characters) when recording is on; recording can be switched off per account (owner / admin). Recordings, transcripts and events are kept **`CALL_RETENTION_DAYS` (default 180)** and then purged by the daily `calls-purge` job; every recording access is audited. Confirm wording and retention with the client's legal team (§7 Q5).

## 5. AI disclosure

- **No Indian rule in force** found that mandates disclosing an AI/automated caller; TRAI is **considering** AI-disclosure amendments (Bar & Bench, 2026-03-09). Confidence **low**.
- Recommendation: **disclose by default** (account setting `aiDisclosureEnabled = true`, already in the data model): _"Main <Company> ki taraf se AI assistant bol rahi hoon."_ — it is already in the PoC persona.

## 7. Questions for the client's legal / compliance team

1. Which licence does the lender hold (bank / NBFC / HFC / other), and which RBI recovery directions apply to them today?
2. Will calls be placed from a **1600-series** number registered to the lender? Who handles DLT / number registration?
3. Confirm the calling window for recovery calls (we default to 09:00–19:00).
4. Maximum call attempts per borrower per day / week?
5. Must every call be recorded? What intimation wording should the bot say? Retention period for recordings and transcripts?
6. Do you want the bot to disclose it is an AI assistant? Exact wording?
7. Lawful basis under DPDP for contacting borrowers (consent / legitimate use) — and for sending voice data to OpenAI (US)? Any data-localisation constraints?
8. Can the bot discuss the amount and dues with whoever answers, or must it first verify the borrower's identity (e.g. date of birth / last 4 digits)?
9. Escalation: when must a human agent take over (dispute, hardship, complaint)?
10. Who signs the DPA, and what breach-notification timeline do you need from us?

## 9. Meta / WhatsApp data (Phase 9)

- **What we receive from Meta:** for each connected WhatsApp number a business token (sealed), the Meta user id that granted access, WABA / number ids, names and Meta statuses of the **business**; webhook events (customer numbers, names and texts) sealed at rest — 7 days, or 30 days for fields later phases import (messages, echoes, chat history, contacts) until Phase 10 stores conversations.
- **Deauthorize callback** (`/api/v1/meta/deauthorize`): the user removed our app in Facebook → that user's numbers need a reconnect; audited (system).
- **Data deletion callback** (`/api/v1/meta/data-deletion`, Meta Platform Terms): deletes what we hold **from Meta for that user** — tokens, PINs, the Meta user id on their numbers — and answers a confirmation code; `/data-deletion?code=…` (public page) shows the status. Business records of the customer's account stay (they are the customer's data; account deletion is Phase 16). Requests are kept 365 days.
- **DPDP:** customer phone numbers in WhatsApp events are personal data of the customer's customers; we are a processor for the customer. Purpose limitation: Phase 9 stores them only for the chat features of Phases 10–12; logs never contain them.
- **Questions for the client's legal team:** (1) Is a 7 / 30-day raw-event retention acceptable before conversations are stored? (2) Which privacy-policy text covers WhatsApp data (Meta App Review checks it)? (3) Should a data deletion request also notify the customer account's owner?

## 10. WhatsApp messages (Phase 10)

- **Retention:** messages and their media are kept **90 days** (`INBOX_RETENTION_DAYS`, owner decision 23 Oct 2026; 0 = keep forever) and deleted daily; conversations and contacts stay. History imported from the WhatsApp Business app is cut at the same limit. Account deletion (Phase 16) removes everything. _Legal review of the 90 days is still open._
- **Opt-out:** a customer's whole-message `STOP` / `UNSUBSCRIBE` / `CANCEL` or Hinglish / Hindi keywords (`BAND KARO`, `MAT BHEJO`, `बंद करो` …, editable per account) opt them out: contact `optedOutAt`, a DND entry, an audit entry and one confirmation reply ("You won't get more messages from us. Reply START to opt in again." + Hinglish). `START` / `SHURU` opt back in (a DND entry a person added stays). Replies inside a window the customer opened stay possible; business-initiated templates (Phase 11) will respect opt-outs.
- **Read receipts** are sent to customers by default (account switch); the typing indicator is sent only when read receipts are on.
- **Voice recording:** agents record voice notes in the browser only while pressing record (browser mic indicator visible); recordings are kept in memory until sent, then stored like any media.
- **DPDP:** WhatsApp messages are personal data processed for the account (the data fiduciary); purpose = customer service on the business's number; access by role; no message text in notifications or logs.

## 11. WhatsApp templates + charges (Phase 11)

- **Marketing consent:** marketing templates are refused for contacts who opted out (STOP keywords), are on the DND list or stopped marketing in WhatsApp (`user_preferences`, error 131050); the builder warns that marketing needs the customer's opt-in (owner decision: a warning, not a hard consent gate). Utility / authentication templates may reach opted-out contacts that are not DND (owner decision) — the legal team should confirm this for loan reminders.
- **Frequency:** at most one marketing template per contact per 24 h (owner default) on top of Meta's own per-user marketing limits (131049).
- **Charges:** customers see the price before sending; a message is charged only when Meta reports it billable on delivery; GST on wallet recharges is unchanged (Phase 4 §8).
- **INR billing:** Meta moves Indian businesses to INR by **31 Dec 2026**; numbers we pay Meta for must be on an INR credit line before then (superadmin page lists the others).

## 12. WhatsApp calling (Phase 12)

- **Permission first:** the business calls a customer only after the customer allowed it in WhatsApp (temporary 7 days or permanent) and Meta confirms it at dial; requests are limited (ours: 1 / 24 h, 2 / 7 days; Meta's too) and the request text is the business's own (reviewed by its admin). Four unanswered calls in a row revoke the permission (Meta's rule).
- **DND / opt-out / window:** DND contacts, opted-out contacts and blocked chats are never called; every business-initiated call respects the account calling window inside TRAI's 09:00–21:00 (agent calls too).
- **Recording notice:** with recording on (account default), the customer hears the account's recording notice before the agent or the AI speaks; recordings follow the Phase 7 retention.
- **Charges:** customers see the per-minute selling price in the wallet; a call is charged in 6-second pulses on Meta's measured duration; customer-initiated calls are free at Meta (only AI usage and a configured platform fee apply).
